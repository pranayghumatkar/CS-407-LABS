--- Shift-invariance diagnostic (add +100 to all logits) ---
    naive softmax at z        : [0.999991, 9e-06, 0.0]
    naive softmax at z+100    : [None, None, None]
    any NaN in naive(z+100)   : True  <- exp(100) overflows
    max |stable(z) - stable(z+100)| = 2.638e-11
    stable matches F.softmax       : True
    Adding a constant c multiplies numerator and denominator by exp(c),
    so the ratio is unchanged mathematically.  In floating point,
    exp(large) overflows to +inf (and inf/inf = NaN), so implementations
    subtract the row maximum first: exp(z - max z) is in (0, 1] and safe.

--- What p - y means ---

    example 0 one-hot y   : [1.0, 0.0, 0.0]
    p - y                 : [-9e-06, 9e-06, 0.0]
    With softmax plus cross-entropy the gradient of the loss with respect
    to the logits is exactly p - y, so a confident-but-wrong class pushes
    that logit down and the correct logit up in proportion to the error.
