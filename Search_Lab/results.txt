--- Task 5: BFS vs A* on the warehouse ---
    measure                  BFS    A* (manhattan)
    --------------------------------------------
    solution found          True              True
    path length               40                40
    states expanded           63                63
    Both find a shortest path, and here both expand the same number of
    states: the warehouse is essentially a single winding corridor, so
    there is little branching for the heuristic to prune.  A*'s advantage
    appears when the map has many alternative branches.

--- Task 5b: A* vs BFS on an open map (many branches) ---
    measure                  BFS    A* (manhattan)
    --------------------------------------------
    path length               13                13
    states expanded           49                13
    Same-length path, but A* expands far fewer states here.

--- Task 6: heuristic investigation on the warehouse ---
    heuristic            found    length    expanded
    manhattan             True        40          63
    zero (h=0)            True        40          63
    euclidean             True        40          63
    manhattan x2          True        40          67
    h=0 removes the heuristic (A* degrades to uniform-cost search);
    euclidean is a weaker admissible lower bound; manhattan x2 is
    inadmissible (too aggressive) and expands more states.
